## Jim's decision (2026-11-20)

Split the test before the `1.0.0` version commit. The tasks are renumbered: task 30 is this task, task 31 the `1.0.0`
version commit, task 32 the showcase README. Recorded in `ROADMAP.md` (the decisions, the checklist, the progress list

## Why

`CLAUDE.md`'s target is every job under 21 minutes. A slow runner put PHP 8.5 `http (2)` at 20:25, and one test,
`test_dragon_specialty_authority_matrix`, was about a sixth of that job. The shard balancer places whole tests, so it
could not move part of that one to another shard.

## The split (`tests/tooling/test_web_application.py`)

- `_dragon_specialty_authority_round(f, spec, level)`, beside `_specialty_accounting_fixture()`, holds the old test's
  body for one specialty at one level, moved without a change but its indentation: the accepted round in
  `subTest(specialty, level)` with all its assertions; the 21 refused cases, each in `subTest(specialty, level, case)`
  with its status (409, 403 or 400) and its `try`/`finally` cleanup; and above level 1 the two insufficient-authority
  checks. `module` is read from the fixture's `modules` map, as the old loop did.
- `test_dragon_specialty_authority_signed_out` is the old test's signed-out block as it was: every op, with and without
  forged fields, 302, 303 or 403, and nothing changed.
- Twelve tests, one per specialty and level, each entering the fixture and calling the helper:
  `test_dragon_specialty_authority_{dark_arts,mystical_powers,thieving_skills}_level_{1,2,3,5}`.
- One test per specialty was the minimum. At the slow run's speed a specialty's test would take about 110 s (329.4 s
  for three), over the 90 s limit, so each specialty is split by level. The fixture is unchanged.
