## 2. LOGINTIMEOUT bounds

**1.1.2** (`configuration.php` line 384 at the tag): `"LOGINTIMEOUT"=>"Seconds of inactivity before auto-logoff,int"`,
any whole number; `common.php` line 177 signs a player out when their last hit is older than now minus the timeout, so 0
signed everyone out on their next page load.

**Now:**

- `lib/settings.php`: `resurrection_bounded_settings()` (`LOGINTIMEOUT` => 300 to 86400) and
  `resurrection_bounded_setting()`. `getsetting()` returns a bounded setting's stored value clamped into its range (a
  value that is not a number reads as the low end), so every reader (`common.php`, `lib/forcednavigation.php`, the
  online lists, PvP) sees 300 for a stored 0.
- `lib/configuration_descriptors.php`: the declaration takes its bounds from that table
  (`int,300,86400`). `src/Http/SettingDescriptor.php`: an `int` declaration may name bounds; a value outside them is
  refused with code `OUT_OF_BOUNDS` and the one line `Not saved: "<label>" must be a whole number from LOW to HIGH.` An
  `int` without bounds is as before.
- `lib/typed_editor.php`: a bounded field is a `type="number"` input with `min` and `max`, and a line under it (bound to it
  by `aria-describedby`): "From 300 to 86400 seconds (5 minutes to 1 day). Below that, players are signed out almost at
  once; above it, a closed browser stays signed in for days. A stored value outside the range is used as the nearest end
  of it."
- `configuration.php`: a save refused for a bound answers 400 with Game Settings itself, the refusal line above the form
  (`role="alert"`), and nothing saved. Every other refusal stays the plain 400 "Invalid or stale settings.".
- `assets/ui/resurrection.css`: `rz-setting-bounded`, `rz-setting-bounds`, `rz-setting-refused`. No inline style.

**Tests**, each failing first at `01cd708`:

- `tests/unit/BoundedSettingsTest.php` (3 tests): `getsetting()` clamps 11 stored values and leaves other settings
  alone; a bounded declaration refuses 0, 299, 86401 and -1 with the one line and accepts 300 and 86400, a non-number is
  refused as before, and bad bounds are refused; Game Settings declares `LOGINTIMEOUT` from the game's table.
  At `01cd708`: 3 failures (`Failed asserting that '0' is identical to 300.`; the declaration had min -2147483647 and
  max 2147483647; the declaration line not found).
- `test_web_application.WebApplicationTests.test_logintimeout_is_bounded_http`: the field and its range line render; a
  POST of 0, 299, 86401 or -5 answers 400 with exactly the one line and changes no setting; 300, 86400 and 900 save; a
  row forced to 0 leaves a signed-in player signed in across two page loads 2.2 s later, `loggedin` still 1. At
