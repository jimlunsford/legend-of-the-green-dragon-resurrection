### The license sections

The brief named section 4(c) for the credit. In CC BY-NC-SA 2.0, **section 4(d) is the credit clause and 4(c) is the
noncommercial clause** (4(a) and 4(b) forbid terms that alter or restrict the license; 7(a) ends it on a breach). This
task uses 4(d) for the credit and 4(c) for the noncommercial term, and said so to Jim when it started. The quoted text
was checked word for word against the legal code at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.0/legalcode.
`NOTICE` quotes 4(c)'s first sentence and 4(d) in full, and `tests/tooling/test_credit_wording.py` holds the 4(d) text
and checks that `NOTICE` carries it exactly.
